<h2>v0.0.0-beta.40 - Blood feel fixes</h2>

<p>Follow-up to beta.39 fixing how blood behaves and reads in-game.</p>

<h3>Fix</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>You no longer get stuck standing in blood.</strong> The fluid copied the original's extreme thickness values (13&times; lava viscosity); dialled down to something only a bit heavier than water.</li>
<li><strong>Blood buckets look like blood.</strong> They now render tinted with the fluid's colour (red / green) instead of showing a plain water bucket.</li>
<li><strong>The Blood Grate shows its fill.</strong> A layer of blood now rises inside the grate as it fills 0&rarr;3, with a sound when it tops out so you know a bottle can be taken.</li>
<li><strong>Blood actually drips downward now</strong> &mdash; falling particles stream from the carcass to whatever is below (grate, floor, up to 6 blocks), with a splash where they land. Puddles land on the real ground instead of floating in the gap.</li>
<li><strong>Taxidermy Table</strong> no longer overhangs into the next block &mdash; its model was ~2 blocks wide, now it fits one.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Known issue</h3>

<ul>
<li>A carcass hung on a hook snaps from its hanging pose to a lying pose when it finishes draining. The original had dedicated hanging-drain models that this port doesn't include yet.</li>
</ul>
