<h2>v0.0.0-beta.33 - Humanoid corpse cutting + the Freezer</h2>

<p>The corpse system gets its real cutting mechanic, and the first of the 4 GUI workstations arrives: the Freezer.</p>

<h3>Add</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Real cutting for all 9 humanoid corpse mobs</strong> (zombie, skeleton, drowned, husk, vindicator, evoker, witch, piglin, piglin brute) — hang the corpse and cut it up in 8 stages: head &gt; skin &gt; 3 meat cuts (per-mob loot tables) &gt; 3 organs from a shared table. Previously corpses had no cutting logic at all.</li>
<li><strong>12 new organ items</strong> (heart, intestines, kidney, liver, lungs, stomach + rotten variants) with models, textures and names — they were referenced by loot tables since earlier betas but never actually registered.</li>
<li><strong>Freezer</strong> — first GUI machine of the mod: a 27-slot cold-storage container with its own screen. Right-click opens the lid (with an iron-trapdoor sound and puffs of freezing smoke particles); closing the GUI shuts it again. Sneak-open skips the animation, just like the original. Emits a comparator signal based on its contents. Craftable (2 iron ingots + 5 white concrete + 1 redstone).</li>
<li><strong>New particle type</strong>: <code>freezersmoke</code>, ported 1:1 from the original with all 8 sprite frames.</li>
<li>All 6 visual states of the Freezer blockstate are available (only closed/open are used in normal play, same as the original).</li>
</ul>

<h3>Total content ported</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>74 mobs</strong> wired to the butchering loop (51 simple animals, 10 humanoids with corpse/organ drops, 4 special-cased mobs, 11 cat coat variants)</li>
<li><strong>38 blocks</strong> from the original catalog (carcasses, heads/trophies, decorative + mechanical)</li>
<li><strong>246 cooking recipes</strong> (smelting/smoking/campfire) for every raw cut</li>
<li>1 GUI workstation (Freezer) — 3 remain</li>
</ul>

<p><em>Still missing: Meat Grinder, Pestle &amp; Mortar and Taxidermy Table (GUI), visible blood, the Iron Golem set (permanently out of scope), and real tool-tier textures (still placeholders). Also known: ~68% of item names still show untranslated keys.</em></p>
