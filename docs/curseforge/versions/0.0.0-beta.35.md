<h2>v0.0.0-beta.35 - Farm animals only</h2>

<p>Big simplification: the mod now covers only farm animals (13 mobs). Plus fixes for missing textures/translations.</p>

<h3>Changed (BREAKING)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Scope cut to 13 farm mobs.</strong> Removed ~64 families for hostile, strange, nether and end mobs (the 10 humanoid corpses with their organ system, all 11 cat variants, axolotls, and 40+ wild/hostile mobs). Kept: cow, pig, sheep, chicken, rabbit, goat, horse, donkey, mule, and 4 llama variants. Orphaned item definitions and creative-tab entries cleaned up with them.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Fix</h3>
<ul>
<li>6 items that had legacy pre-1.21.4 model definitions directly in <code>items/</code> (chicken legs/wings, bird foot, wishbone, silverfish/endermite chunks) now correctly split into <code>models/item/</code> geometry + <code>items/</code> definition — they were showing as missing despite having textures.</li>
<li>154 missing translations added for remaining farm items (carcass/head/skeleton/skin variants were showing raw keys like <code>item.slaughter_hide.goat_carcass</code>).</li>
<li>681 out-of-bounds UVs clamped across 95 custom models (closed freezer, every skin_rack variant, all head mounts) — these threw <code>Cannot compute translucency out of bounds</code> in 26.2 and killed whole model bakes.</li>
</ul>
