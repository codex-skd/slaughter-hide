<h2>v0.0.0-beta.37 - Butcher's workshop: 4 machines, the blood system, and a full kill-to-cut loop</h2>

<p>The biggest beta so far. Every butchering machine from the original mod is now in, the blood system is implemented, and the whole "kill an animal &rarr; hang it &rarr; bleed it &rarr; drain it &rarr; cut it up &rarr; cook it" loop is wired end to end for all 7 supported mobs (cow, pig, sheep, chicken, rabbit, goat, polar bear). A large amount of dead content left over from the earlier 77-mob scope has also been swept out.</p>

<blockquote><strong>This is a beta.</strong> The GUIs, the machine recipes and the blood fluid rendering have <em>not</em> been smoke-tested in a running client yet &mdash; please report anything that looks wrong.</blockquote>

<br>
<hr>
<br>

<h3>Butchering machines (GUI blocks)</h3>

<p>All four of the original mod's machine blocks with real menus are now ported. Each opens a screen, keeps its contents when you close it, and outputs a redstone comparator signal.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Meat Grinder</strong> &mdash; grinds meat into other products: a carcass block &rarr; meat scraps; raw pork + intestines + a sausage attachment &rarr; a raw sausage (add a bottle of blood for a raw blood sausage); raw mutton &rarr; raw lamb mince; raw beef &rarr; raw beef mince. Comes with 8 new input/output items (intestines, sausage attachment, meat scraps, raw sausage, raw blood sausage, raw beef/lamb mince, bottle of blood).</li>
<li><strong>Pestle and Mortar</strong> &mdash; crushes things: a hoof &rarr; a slime ball; any crushable bone (vanilla bone, bird foot, wishbone) &rarr; bone meal.</li>
<li><strong>Taxidermy Table</strong> &mdash; turns a hide + a head + planks into a mounted trophy for each of the 7 mobs (chicken and rabbit have no hide, so it's head + 2 planks). Crafted from an animal skin over planks.</li>
<li><strong>Freezer</strong> &mdash; already present, unchanged.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Blood system</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Two custom fluids</strong>: Blood and Infected Blood, with source/flowing states, their own fluid physics, buckets, and in-world rendering.</li>
<li><strong>Blood Grate</strong> &mdash; place it under a carcass that is bleeding and it fills up over three animals. Right-click a full grate with a glass bottle to collect a Bottle of Blood (which feeds the Meat Grinder's blood-sausage recipe).</li>
<li><strong>Blood Puddle</strong> &mdash; a thin ground layer that appears under a bleeding carcass when there is no grate, and dries up over time.</li>
<li>Bleeding a carcass now leaves visible blood (grate fill or a puddle) instead of a placeholder smoke puff.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Kill-to-cut loop completed for all 7 mobs</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Cooked Polar Bear Meat</strong> added (the raw item existed with nowhere to go) &mdash; smelting, smoking and campfire recipes.</li>
<li><strong>Cooked Chicken Leg</strong> and <strong>Cooked Chicken Wing</strong> added (present in the original mod, previously missing here).</li>
<li>Polar bear now correctly replaces its vanilla cod <em>and</em> salmon drops with the carcass.</li>
<li>Added the two missing drained-carcass block loot tables (goat, polar bear).</li>
<li>Verified pig / sheep / chicken / rabbit / goat against the original: definitions, loot tables, blockstates and assets all match.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Polar bear fixes</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Mounted Polar Bear Head</strong> now has a texture &mdash; the block was never actually being registered.</li>
<li>Fixed the missing Polar Bear Head Mount / Skeleton item models and the magenta Drained Polar Bear Carcass texture.</li>
<li>Added all the missing polar bear translations (EN + ES).</li>
</ul>

<h3>Goat fixes</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Goat Skin</strong> now has a texture.</li>
<li>Removed 6 phantom goat food items that were not in the original mod and were unobtainable (the goat shares the sheep's lamb cuts).</li>
</ul>

<h3>Cleanup</h3>

<ul>
<li>Swept <strong>1,822 orphan asset and data files</strong> left over from the old 77-mob scope (models, textures, blockstates, loot tables, recipes for mobs and furniture that are no longer in the mod). Everything that is actually registered is preserved.</li>
<li>Removed <strong>28 unobtainable meat and organ items</strong> for out-of-scope mobs, plus their models, textures, translations and one recipe.</li>
<li>Removed 3 dead block classes, 22 orphan translation keys, and 7 stray migration scripts committed by mistake.</li>
<li>The in-game JAR dropped from ~2.9 MB to ~1.3 MB.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Known gaps in this beta</h3>

<ul>
<li>Blood fluid rendering compiles against the Minecraft 26.2 API but has not been checked in a running client yet.</li>
<li>Blood bucket items use the vanilla water-bucket texture as a placeholder; Bottle of Infected Blood reuses the Bottle of Blood texture.</li>
<li>Blood puddles do not spread; the Blood Grate has no cross-mod blood-magic/vampirism compatibility.</li>
<li>The Taxidermy Table recipes are a small hand-made set (the original's recipe data is not in the extractable assets).</li>
<li>The machines do not port the original's custom JEI recipe categories.</li>
</ul>
